SE15 4London Borough of Southwark

Peckham Rye and Bellenden

Postcode sector, 3,412 homes, 7,960 residents

Busy, well-connected corner of south-east London with Overground and Thameslink trains, a big park, and prices just above the London average. Crime is higher than most of England, mainly around Rye Lane.
