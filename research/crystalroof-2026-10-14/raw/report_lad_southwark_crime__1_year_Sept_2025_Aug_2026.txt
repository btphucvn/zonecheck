Is Southwark a safe place to live?

Based on police-recorded crime, Southwark has a relatively high crime rate. It records 121 crimes per 1,000 at-risk population a year, giving it a high crime score of 9/10 compared with other local authority districts in England and Wales.

In Southwark, elevated crime scores are seen across the vast majority of crime types.

Although Southwark has an overall high crime score of 9/10, crime levels are distributed unevenly across the area, with local area scores ranging from a low of 2/10 to a high of 10/10. Low, medium and high-scoring local areas are represented in similar numbers. The map shows where each local area sits.

The safest wards in Southwark are Champion Hill (4/10 low), Dulwich Wood (4/10 low) and Peckham Rye (5/10 medium).

The least safe wards in Southwark are North Walworth (9/10 high), Rye Lane (8/10 high) and Faraday (8/10 high).

In Southwark, overall crime levels are very high, with elevated scores recorded across a wide range of offence categories.

Highest relative crime scores

Robbery scores 10/10. This means street-level theft involving confrontation forms a notable pressure point in the area.

Anti-social behaviour scores 9/10. This indicates that day-to-day disorder is a visible feature of the area.

Theft from the person also scores 9/10. This suggests elevated levels of personal theft in public spaces.

Burglary also scores 9/10. This suggests that property security is the primary pressure point in the area.

Other theft also scores 9/10. This means opportunistic theft presents a pressure point in the area.

Bicycle theft also scores 9/10. This points to cycle security as a recurring concern locally.

Drugs scores 8/10. Drug-related activity represents a recurring concern in the area.

Vehicle crime scores 7/10. Vehicle security represents a key pressure point in the area.

Most common crimes by volume

Violence and sexual offences (25.2 per 1,000 at-risk population, 4/10), Anti-social behaviour (23.6 per 1,000 at-risk population, 9/10) and Theft from the person (17.5 per 1,000 at-risk population, 9/10) are the most common crime types by volume in Southwark.
