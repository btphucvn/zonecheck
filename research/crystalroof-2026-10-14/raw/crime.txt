Is Adys Road, London, SE15 4DX a safe place to live?

Based on police-recorded crime, the local area around Adys Road, London, SE15 4DX has a moderate crime rate. It records 87.8 crimes per 1,000 at-risk population a year, giving it a medium crime score of 6/10 compared with other local areas in England and Wales.

Most crime types in the local area have low to moderate crime scores, although some categories show higher relative levels compared with other areas in England and Wales. Bicycle theft (9/10), Vehicle crime (8/10), Burglary (7/10) and Robbery (7/10) stand out as the highest-scoring crime types in the area.

Looking at the wider area, Southwark records 121 crimes per 1,000 at-risk population, resulting in a high crime score of 9/10 compared with other local authority districts in England and Wales. This means the wider area has higher crime levels than many other local authority districts nationally.
